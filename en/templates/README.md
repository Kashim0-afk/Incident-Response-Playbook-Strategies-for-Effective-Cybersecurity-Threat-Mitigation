# Templates

Copy the template into your ticketing system or document tool and fill in the fields in braces. Remove any section that does not apply rather than writing "N/A" everywhere.

| Template | When to use it |
|---|---|
| [Internal communication](internal-communication.md) | First notice to staff, updates, management briefing |
| [Notification to CSIRT / authorities](authority-notification.md) | Drafting the NIS2 early warning, notification and final report, and the GDPR notification to the Garante |
| [Post-incident report](post-incident-report.md) | Blameless review after SEV1 and SEV2 incidents |
